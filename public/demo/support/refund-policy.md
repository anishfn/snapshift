# Lumen refund policy (internal)

Lumen is a notes app sold as a subscription. Support agents follow these rules. When two rules apply, the one
higher in the list wins.

1. **Chargeback filed.** If the customer has already disputed the charge with their bank, we do not refund it:
   the bank decides. Send the ticket to the Billing lead.
2. **Duplicate charge.** Two identical charges for the same plan on the same day are always a mistake on our
   side. Refund one of them in full, at any time, once the duplicate is confirmed on a statement.
3. **Charged after a failed payment.** If checkout said the payment failed and the card was charged anyway,
   refund in full.
4. **Monthly plans.** Full refund within 14 days of the charge. After 14 days, no refund; the plan can be
   cancelled so it does not renew.
5. **Annual plans.** Full refund within 30 days of the charge. From day 31 to day 90, a pro-rated refund for the
   unused months. After 90 days, no refund.
6. **Add-ons.** AI credit packs are refunded only if none of the credits have been used.
7. **Large refunds.** Any refund over $500 needs a manager's approval before it is sent.

Refunds go back to the original card and take 5 to 10 business days to show up.
