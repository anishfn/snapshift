# anishfn.ink catalog

A personal catalog of components and tools Anish has made, shown as a dense masonry wall of live, interactive blocks of mixed heights. Every block is the real thing, not a screenshot.

Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router, read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using unfamiliar APIs), React 19, Tailwind v4, TypeScript, `motion` (`motion/react`). Package manager: **bun**.

**This file is a living record of Anish's taste.** When they correct something, praise something, or ask for a change that reveals a preference, update the relevant section and add a dated line to the taste log at the bottom. Prefer refining an existing rule over appending a contradicting one.

## Taste

What Anish has asked for, and therefore what every change must respect:

- **Plain, minimal, rounded.** Visual reference is shwn.design: take its fonts, palette and feel, never its images or assets.
- **The blocks are the page.** No titles, years or captions under blocks. A block's name appears only on hover, as a small borderless pill in the bottom-left corner, one shade darker than the block.
- **One width everywhere: 1200px.** The wall, its header and footer, and every piece page (header, stage, info) share the same 1200px column and edges; `scrollbar-gutter: stable` keeps those edges identical between short and long pages. The wall is a masonry: every block has its own height (`pieces/wall-heights.ts`), 1 to 4 columns (about 290px wide at full width), filled left to right into the shortest column (`components/masonry.ts`).
- **One quiet top bar, with a mark, not sticky.** It scrolls away with the page. Left, one line on every page: the mark (`Mark` in `site.tsx`: unavatar's pixel smile, the "@_" anonymous face, redrawn on a 16-unit grid so at 16px every bar (eyes, nose, smile) is the same 2px and lands on whole pixels, the smile's bottom 8 units wide so it reads as a smile at 1.25x scaling too, 16px so it sits with "lab", one tone `neutral-900`, no tile or background, static: no wink or hover effect), "lab" (15px medium), a `neutral-300` slash, then where you are in `neutral-600`: the tagline on the wall ("components and tools I made", the page's h1) or the piece's title (and "lab" links home). Anish found "lab" over a two-line tagline "mid", a big logo "too big", and rejected a dark tile with a 2×2 grid and a flask. Right on the wall: `KindFilter`, the search field (`/` or ⌘K), then the icon links as one 36px group (12px radius, 3px inset, 30px buttons at 9px): portfolio (globe) and X. Text and URLs live in `src/lib/site.ts`. The favicon (`app/icon.svg`, light and dark), `favicon.ico` and `apple-icon.png` use the bolder original smile.
- **Opening a piece is one movement** (Anish: "the transition is BAD, check yourself slowly"). Shared elements, never stretched snapshots: the surface (`share="morph"`) is drawn by the view transition group itself (grey, 24px radius, clipped) and grows block → stage, the block's snapshot fades at once and the stage's contents (toolbars) fade in late at their own size; the artwork (`pieces/art.tsx`, `share="art"`: the folder, the icon grid, the cube and camera frames) is the same drawing at the same aspect ratio on both sides, so it simply grows. The header parts (`vt-brand`, `vt-links`, `vt-crumb`, `vt-controls`) and the page bodies (`vt-wall-body`, `vt-piece-body`, one-sided names) are named only during typed transitions (`open`, `home`, `nav-*`): Suspense reveals are view transitions too and made the header flicker. The wall controls rise 12px and fade (220ms); the new page's text settles in after (300ms, 160ms delay, 8px). Heavy set-up (three.js scenes, the folder's grain and first paint) waits for `afterTransition` (`@/lib/after-transition`, `document.activeViewTransition.finished`): it held the transition's first frame for 170 to 300ms. Check transitions frame by frame at 1/10 speed (CDP `Animation.setPlaybackRate`) and with long-animation-frame timings, several runs (headless timings are noisy).
- **Moving between pieces keeps what's shared still** ("we don't need to render all of it again"): the header, the stage surface (`nav-stage`) and the Share and arrow controls (`nav-controls`) hold; the piece slides inside the still stage and the title (`nav-title`) and text slide, 24px with fade and blur.
- **Copy reads as ownership.** First person, these are things Anish made ("Components and tools I've made, to my taste"). Lowercase-casual is fine, never salesy.
- **No em dashes anywhere**: UI copy, titles, metadata, code comments. Use a comma, a period, or `·`.
- **Mono only for code.** Commands and code use JetBrains Mono (`font-mono`). Everything else, numbers included, is the sans with `tabular-nums`.
- **Icons are the site's own: solid, rounded, single tone** (see Icons). Chevrons for navigation, never arrows.
- **Not just components.** Tools Anish built live here too. The line between them: a **tool** makes something you take away (Folder maker's icon, Handdrawn frames' framed photo, the pixel art model); a **component** is a piece of interface you'd put on a site (My icons, Video cube, Action camera, Morfe card, Dia gradient).
- **Footer mark**: 24px beside the two-line "© year / anishfn" (32px was too big, 16px too small next to two lines).
- **Rounded squares, not pills.** Controls use the site's radius scale, never `rounded-full` (only a switch track/knob may be a pill, and buttons that pop out of a round object, like the Knob HUD's, are round like it): 24 for blocks and the stage, 16 for cards, 12 for 36px controls (search, link buttons), 10 for 32px buttons and tab groups, 6 for 24px keys, tabs, chips and buttons inset in a 10. Inner radius always = outer minus inset.
- **Concentric radius is checked by eye.** Anish spots mismatched nesting immediately (a square key inside a round search pill). Any control inside a rounded container must use inner radius = outer radius minus the inset, with an even inset on all sides.
- **Responsive.** The top bar wraps (line wraps, never truncates; on phones search drops to its own full-width row). The wall goes 1→5 columns. A piece's stage is `h-[clamp(400px,54svh,480px)]` on phones and `sm:h-[clamp(440px,64svh,680px)]` above: low enough that the page under it shows (Anish found a 72vh stage too tall on a phone).
- **Controls never arrive into an empty stage.** Every piece's control bars are wrapped in `Arriving` (`pieces/arriving.tsx`): a `skeleton-control` placeholder (a shade darker than the stage, the slow sheen) in the bar's exact shape, there from the first paint and gone once the bar's entrance completes. New pieces wrap their bars the same way; bars that render visible from the first paint (Pixel art's) need none. The plain `skeleton` is the stage's own grey, so on a stage only its sheen showed.
- **The title row never wraps on a phone**: Share is its icon alone below `sm`, so a long title ("Handdrawn frames") keeps Share and the arrows beside it.
- **Nothing pops.** Every element that appears or disappears blur-fades (`iconSwap` inside `AnimatePresence`), including tiny ones like the search pill's `/` key. Conditional rendering without a transition reads as broken.
- **Every piece has its own page** at `/<slug>` (`src/app/[slug]/page.tsx`, prebuilt; `components/piece-page.tsx`). Blocks on the wall are real links (cmd-click works); no overlay viewer and no drag between pieces. The block morphs into the page's stage (React `<ViewTransition name="piece-<slug>" share="morph">` on both, 420ms ease-out with a touch of blur). Under the stage: the title (the page's h1), a Share button (copies the link; the share sheet on touch devices), prev/next arrows in one control group, the description, then Info, the prompt and the install card, and prev/next cards with names at the end (side by side on every width, phones included). ←/→ move between pieces unless a field or the 3D stage is using the arrows. Live pieces are imported directly (`pieces/live.ts`) so the page server-renders them with their controls in place from the first paint; the wall (`pieces/index.ts`) only imports light stand-ins (`Wall`). Each route has its own skeleton (`(home)/loading.tsx`, `[slug]/loading.tsx`).
- **Don't wrap things in extra boxes.** Anish dislikes containers around containers ("don't add boxes"). Reach for spacing and alignment before adding a card or panel.
- **No placeholder blocks.** The wall shows only real pieces; never pad it with empty blocks.

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page background | `#fbfbfb` (`bg-background`) | from shwn.design |
| Body text | `#707070` (`text-foreground`) | headings and active states use `neutral-900` |
| Block surface | `#f2f2f2` (`bg-surface`) | radius `24px` |
| Hover label | `#e4e4e4` fill, `neutral-600` text, 12px, 24px tall, `rounded-[12px]`, 12px inset | no border; concentric with the 24px block |
| Controls on a block | `#e7e7e7` fill | pills, round icon buttons |
| Raised white surfaces | `bg-white shadow-surface` | cards, inputs, lists inside a block |
| Popovers | `bg-white shadow-popover` | dropdowns, toasts, menus |
| Sans | Public Sans (`font-sans`) | everything |
| Hand | Caveat (`font-hand`, not preloaded) | tiny annotations like "made with care" |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono (`font-mono`, not preloaded) | code and commands only |

- Use the shadow tokens (`shadow-surface`, `shadow-popover`, defined in `src/app/globals.css`), not new arbitrary shadows. Bespoke shadows are allowed only for physical objects (knob, folder, switch thumb, clock face).
- **Concentric radius**: outer radius = inner radius + padding (e.g. `rounded-2xl p-1` wraps `rounded-xl`).
- Prefer shadows to borders for depth; keep borders only for dividers and focus/selected states.
- **Hover on small controls is a soft darker fill** (`hover:bg-black/[0.06]`), never a white fill with a ring or shadow: a bordered circle appearing on hover looks bad.
- Accent color is almost absent. Black (`neutral-900`) is the accent. Rose/emerald appear only for meaning (like, error, success, call buttons).

## Layout

- `src/app/page.tsx` renders `src/components/catalog.tsx` (client): top bar (not sticky) with search, then the masonry: one CSS grid of 4px rows, each block's column and row precomputed for 1/2/3/4 columns (sm/lg/xl, rem breakpoints; never mix px arbitrary breakpoints with rem ones) and picked by breakpoint, so the server renders the final layout and nothing moves. The skeleton (`(home)/loading.tsx`) uses the same placement. Then a small footer.
- `src/components/block.tsx`: the block shell; its height comes from the masonry (`style` from `masonry()`). No entrance animation: blocks and their content render in place, never fade or slide in on load or scroll. `title` and `children` are optional: a block without them is an empty placeholder with no hover label.
- At five columns a block is about 254px wide: **a piece must fit in 240px wide.**

## Adding a piece

1. Add its data to `src/components/pieces/meta.ts` (slug, title, description, npm dependencies, source files). The description shows in the viewer and in the shadcn registry, so write it in first person-adjacent plain language, no em dashes.
2. Create `src/components/pieces/<name>.tsx`: `"use client"`, one named export, no props. Import shared code by alias only (`@/components/icons`, `@/lib/motion`), never relative paths, so the registry copy works in other projects. Only use theme tokens the kit ships (`shadow-surface`, `shadow-popover`, `bg-surface`); add new ones to `themeTokens([...])` in `scripts/build-registry.ts`. It renders centered inside the block, so no outer margins (a negative top margin is fine when a popover needs room to open downward).
3. Put its slug at the **front** of `order` in meta.ts: the wall is newest first, and every new piece appears at the top. Register it in `src/components/pieces/index.ts`: spread its `meta` entry and add `slug`, `title` (shown in the hover pill), `size`, `Component`.
4. **Tools** set `kind: "tool"` in meta.ts (components use `"component"`). Tools are used on the site, not copied: no files, no shadcn install, skipped by the registry. Heavy tools give a static `Preview` for the wall and the viewer's side peeks; the full `Component` only mounts in the viewer. Tools that live elsewhere may also add `href`. The hover pill becomes a link with an arrow-up-right icon. The block itself should still show something live or meaningful about the tool, not a logo on a gray square.
5. Give it a wall height in `pieces/wall-heights.ts` (a multiple of 4px). Heights should differ from the neighbours': the wall is a masonry. Shape the wall view to its block: a wide, short block for wide things (the Dia gradient is 168px tall with 7 columns instead of 11 on its page).

### Every piece must

- If it has controls, give feedback on every press (`whileTap={press}`). Showcase pieces (like My icons) stay fully static: no hover effects, no entrance animation, nothing to click.
- Work with keyboard: real `<button>`s, `role`/`aria-*` for switches, sliders, tabs, radios; arrow keys on sliders/knobs.
- Fit the palette above and use icons from `src/components/icons.tsx`.
- Avoid hydration mismatches: no `Date.now()`/random in render. Use `useSyncExternalStore` with a `null` server snapshot for live values (see `clock.tsx`).
- Pass `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` (the React Compiler lint rules forbid reading refs during render and calling setState synchronously in effects).

## Tools

- **Folder maker** (`pieces/folder-maker.tsx`): no drawing. A macOS-style folder (measured from the real one: S-curve tab, rounded front, translucent pocket band, leather grain), macOS blue by default. Upload or drop a photo: the folder takes its dominant color and the image is tinted, grained and lit like the folder; drag (or arrow keys) to reposition it. One toolbar (16 outer, 6 inset, 10 buttons: Choose/Change, Download, dark primary "Share to the wall"). Sharing opens a dialog for an optional X username; the folder is saved to Vercel Blob at `folders/<username or _>/` and appears in the viewer's "From the wall" 3×3 gallery (`folder-gallery.tsx`, nine more per "Show more") with an avatar + @username chip linked to x.com. Avatars come from unavatar.io.
- **Video cube** (`pieces/video-cube.tsx`): the TwelveLabs cuboids, matched closely to their illustration (Anish sent it and asked to "check closely what a cuboid is like and how the gradient looks"). Four long, wide, low, pillowy rounded blocks (long side clearly longer than the clip's end, the end itself broad, sides about half the width tall; Anish: "made the cube long which is good, but also make it wider"), in a staggered 2×2: pink top left, lavender top right, orange and mint below; drawn back to front mint, lavender, orange, pink. Each block has its **own light gradient** running corner to corner and fading to nearly white (pink and lavender pale at the far end, orange and mint strongest there; a cream toward the clip), a light lift on top, no hard edges between faces, a soft halo of its color. Each clip (surf, wild horses, cherry blossom, mossy waterfall; Mixkit) sits on the short end out to its right edge, feathering into the cream toward the long side. Rendered in three passes (silhouettes into r, g, b and a; a gaussian blur; a composite in draw slots), colors passed as raw sRGB values (through three's color management they came out heavy and dark). Controls: colors (TwelveLabs, Dawn, Ocean, Mono, Custom with a color per block in the shared selector), Float, play/pause; and Softness. Only the grid: Steps and Stack layouts were tried and removed ("just remove the stack and steps, only stay grid"). No download, no hover tilt. three.js loads only on the page; the wall and the first paint show one transparent still (`video-cube-frame.webp`), captured on black and on white to recover its exact alpha; re-capture it whenever the look changes.
- **Action camera** (`pieces/action-camera.tsx`): a GoPro-style camera in real 3D (three.js primitives in GoPro proportions, laid out like the real one: small screen upper left, the lens housing up in the top right corner with a big round glass, bezel, inner ring and pupil), drawn as a clean pencil product sketch (like a LEGO construction sheet), not a shaded model. Rendered in three passes (surface direction and depth, part ids, then graphite): straight graphite lines (no wobble) with the paper's tooth, the contour slightly darker than the inner lines, each edge one line (every test fires on one side only), a whisper of fine hatching on the darkest faces, no fills. Light construction lines are real 3D lines: the box edges carried well past the corners, centre lines across front, top and side, a diagonal through the lens. Drag turns it freely about the screen's axes with inertia, hover tilts it, arrows turn it, Home or Reset view eases back to the isometric pose. Same handover pattern as the Video cube (one full-frame still for wall and stage, CSS-sized stage).
- **Morfe card** (`pieces/morfe-card*.ts(x)`): the Morfe waitlist's member card (designed by Sebastian Dahiya in Figma, made live by Anish), ported from `~/YC/morfe-waitlist`. A WebGL2 slab (`morfe-card-gl.ts`, its own shader: stock, print grain, a pearly coat, the wordmark blind-pressed into the back from a height map) fed by 2048px canvas textures (`morfe-card-raster.ts`) from a shared layout (`morfe-card-layout.ts`). Tilts toward the pointer, tap or Enter turns it, picking a painting (Rings, Stripes, Waves) wipes it across. Assets in `public/pieces/morfe-card` (paintings re-encoded to 1200px, Geist faces as woff2, Anish's X photo). Same handover pattern: one still (`morfe-card-frame.webp`, the canvas frame with its shadow, captured transparent from the live card) for the wall and the first paint; the loop hides it in the same frame it first draws. Set-up waits for `afterTransition`.
- **Dia gradient** (`pieces/dia-gradient*.tsx`): designed by Sebastian Dahiya in Figma for Morfe, made live by Anish. Eleven blurred columns of one gradient in a stepped pyramid that follows the pointer while it's over the piece; sized in container units; colors are CSS variables motion eases between. The page has a palette bar at the top of the stage (Morfe, Dusk, Lagoon, Peach, Mono, Custom with five colors in the shared selector) and a "Columns − 11 +" bar at the foot (3 to 25; a new count crossfades in and rises, the number blur-swaps). Not installable (Anish removed the shadcn card).
- **Handdrawn frames** (`pieces/handdrawn-frames*.ts(x)`, `handdrawn-frame.tsx`): fourteen frames in the style of a reference Anish sent (red ink frames doodled around old childhood photos), drawn by us, never traced from it: Curls, Lace, Scallop, Bows, Wavy, Cameo, Daisy, Beads, Sun, Ripple, Arch, Zigzag, Stitch, Laurel. Every line comes from a seeded generator in `handdrawn-frames-shapes.ts` (wobbling strokes that overshoot corners, curls, scallops, petals, beads, rays, stitches, a laurel stem with alternating leaves), so server and browser draw the same frame. **Rings close exactly on themselves**: their wobble is periodic in angle (whole waves per turn), never a spiral or an overlapping tail, which Anish saw as a stray second line. Openings are rect, ellipse or any path (`openingPath`, `openingBox`). The photo is clipped to the opening and always black and white (an SVG `feColorMatrix` filter, which works in Safari too, not CSS `filter` on SVG). Default photo: Anish's sleeping street cat (`handdrawn-frames-cat.webp`, square crop around the cat). Layout Anish asked for: frames along the top (scrolls sideways on a phone), inks in a vertical bar on the right (Red first, Ink, Blue, Green, Pink, then Custom, which opens our own color selector beside the bar: saturation/brightness square, hue strip, hex field, keyboard sliders, Escape or outside click closes), Choose photo, the zoom (a slider with a step either side) and Download along the foot; dropping a photo anywhere works. The photo is adjustable in its frame: drag to move it, pinch, trackpad pinch (ctrl+wheel) or the slider to zoom 1 to 3x, arrows and +/- from the keyboard, double-click or 0 to reset. Its fit is kept as fractions of the spare room (`placePhoto`) of one shared photo box (`photoBox`: 128×176 around the frame's centre, holding every opening), so the photo stays at exactly the same place on screen when the frame changes (only the window onto it and the lines change, as a plain crossfade, no scale or blur), and the download matches. The frame row also scrolls by mouse drag (a drag never picks a frame) and by mouse wheel while it has room. The frame row shows a fade plus a soft blur at whichever end has more frames past it (`--fade-l`/`--fade-r`, registered with `@property` so they transition), and keeps the picked frame scrolled into view. Bar wrappers are `pointer-events-none` with only the bars catching clicks: the ink bar's full-height wrapper once covered the end of the frame row, so the last frame couldn't be clicked. Download (`handdrawn-frames-export.ts`) saves a 1600×2000 PNG on old album paper ("grandpa bg": warm cream, blotches, foxing, fibres, specks, grain, darker edges), the print pasted in with a soft shadow, the ink multiplied into the paper. A picked frame draws itself in line by line (`pathLength`, 0.6s, staggered within 0.6s total), never on first paint; ink changes run through the lines (300ms).
- **Knob HUD** (`pieces/knob-hud.tsx`; parts and the wall in `knob-hud-parts.tsx`, the remote in `knob-hud-remote.tsx`, the readings in `knob-hud-intents.tsx`, the guide in `knob-hud-notes.tsx`, the orb from `pieces/orbkit/`): built from a design handoff of Knob, a macOS menu-bar app the Siri Remote drives (`design-handoff/`), with **Anish's Shapeshift** (`~/Desktop/for-github/shapeshift`) inside: its offline classifier and parsers ported to `pieces/shapeshift/` (no zod, no `Math.random`), every one of its nineteen cards with a reading and three actions with icons. **Input comes from the remote's mic, not the clipboard**: hold Siri to talk (Web Speech API, push to talk, the card opens when you let go), tap it or the orb to type; Return or the clickpad opens the card ("You said" / "You typed"). No examples on the stage; the composer's empty placeholder cycles through Shapeshift's README lines. **The remote is the main control**: a Siri Remote lying on its side **centred at the top of the stage, the HUD in the bottom-right corner** (centred on a phone), panels opening up into the room between; **Workspaces opens in the centre of the stage**, not over the HUD. Every remote part works (clickpad sides on screen, middle picks, **up opens Workspaces**, Back, TV tap closes and hold opens meta and the timeline, play/pause, mute, +/- button sets, power sleeps, Siri talks) and shows a level label under the remote on hover. **No rings or highlights telling you what to press** ("don't add a guide on what to press"); the only on-stage guidance is one handwritten line over the remote that changes with the state (Anish likes it, but it needs room: 20px above the remote). Actions that keep going stay in the HUD: **Start runs a live timer** (Stop, 5 more min, Pause; Back leaves a counting chip over the orb), Roll again re-rolls, Log one more moves the goal bar; the rest confirm with a toast and close. **Toasts are a quiet glass chip centred just over the orb, only when no panel is open** ("the toast appearing looks bad" as a white card with a check). **The HUD's buttons, panels and toast are the site's raised white** (`raised`: white with `--shadow-surface`), the lit button solid black: Liquid Glass was tried and removed ("don't make the hud buttons liquid glass, keep it as my taste is"). **Only hairline shadows** ("I don't like the shadows, do minimal"). **The orb is Orbkit's SHDR-21** (zzzzshawn, `bunx --bun shadcn@latest view zzzzshawn/orbkit/shdr-21`, vendored in `pieces/orbkit/` with em dashes swapped and a lighter march: 28 steps, 2 light steps, 3 octaves, `maxDpr` 1.25) with **nothing behind it: no disc, no ring, no black border** ("don't add a black border to the orb"). To read on the light stage it's denser (`density` 4.4, `alphaGain` 2.6) and its light holds to one side (`lightSpin: 0`, more ambient per state), so there's always a warm lit limb and a blue shade; it speaks while Knob listens, thinks while you type, pauses asleep or with the tab hidden. Tried and rejected: a CSS pearl, a three.js pearl, a Siri-ribbon shader, a TwelveLabs gradient ("orb looks bad"), SHDR-25 (pale at 76px, "the orb ain't visible"), SHDR-21 on black or navy (a dark spot, then "a black border"). The guide and share card use a still of it (`knob-hud-orb-still.webp`). Buttons are 76px circles, the orb's size, 12px apart, icon over label, labels in 56px; **every label is one line** (the detail goes in the toast: "Paste", then "Pasted ₹800"), so each face centres its icon and label and every icon sits at the same height. Two-line labels could never be right: centred per button the icons jumped ("looks inconsistent"), pinned they sat high ("doesn't look centered"), a row-height block left dead space under one-line labels ("the space below feels bad"). No focus ring on HUD buttons; the lit fill is the focus indicator. The card in the bubble: a small mark and headline, the value as a big 26px figure, facts under it; **lists are one column** (a two-column checklist "looks bad"), round checks, five then "N more"; poll options as stacked rows. Panels have a 6px inset all round (20px corners, 14px inside) with header text level with the field's ("more margin top and sides looks bad"). On touch the composer never focuses itself (no surprise keyboard; a tap on the field does it). Workspaces scales down on a phone and shows only the picked one and its neighbours. The composer is just its header, field and card (a row of keycap hints "looks bad", removed). **The wall thumbnail is only the remote, centred** in a short, wide block (196px); the share card is the stage in small (remote across the top, a lit action, its toast and the orb in the corner), from stills (`knob-hud-remote-still.webp`, `knob-hud-orb-still.webp`, a transparent SHDR-21 frame on the navy disc); re-capture them if the look changes. **Smoothness** ("it feels laggy, I want all smooth, no lag, less space"): no `backdrop-filter` and no blur filters in any of its motion (transform and opacity only), layout animations measured only when the grip changes (`layoutDependency`), the card preview reads a deferred copy of the draft, the running timer ticks in its own components (the HUD only hears its end), tighter padding in the bubble and card. **The orb only animates while it means something** (listening, the composer open, 1.6s after any change so states glide), otherwise it holds its last frame: a resting HUD runs no render loop. Its settings are module constants (new objects each render re-ran its effects), the remote is memoised behind one stable callback (hovering the row re-rendered it), and nothing animates a layout property (the listening bars scale, never resize). Measured in a production build: 60fps at rest, median 16ms and no long tasks while driving it. Under the piece, a guide made of the piece's own parts: the remote numbered with a legend (pointing at an item rings that part on the diagram only), "From a sentence to done" in three pictured steps, "More on the remote" (hold TV, press up, + and -), keyboard keys, credits. Motion follows the Apple design skill.
- **Axolotl pond** (`pieces/axolotl-pond*.ts(x)`): Anish asked for shwn.design's koi pond "with all the effects" but axolotls instead of fish. Rebuilt in our own WebGL2 (`axolotl-pond-gl.ts`) with the same techniques: two wave fields stepped 300 times a second (fine rings, a broad swell bodies push into wakes), a drifting simplex swell, caustics from a refracted grid (area ratio), a painted bed with stone shadows, swaying plants, offset blurred silhouettes for shadows, refraction, colour split in the light, ACES, grain and a wandering soft edge that dissolves into the stage. The axolotls (`axolotl-pond-swim.ts` for behaviour, the shader for looks) are drawn from (u along, s across): broad flat head, beady eyes, a smile crease, three feathery gill stalks a side (separate stalks, not one leafy blob), chunky legs walking in diagonal pairs that fold back for a dart, a translucent fin round the tail; leucistic, golden, wild, copper. ## Motion

Values come from Emil Kowalski's and Jakub Krehel's guidance (the `emil-design-eng` and `better-ui` skills). Shared values live in `src/lib/motion.ts`; import them instead of inventing new ones.

| Use | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Press feedback | `whileTap={press}` → `scale: 0.96`, never below 0.95 |
| Enter/exit/response curve | `easeOut` = `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` (Tailwind `ease-out` is overridden to this) |
| On-screen movement curve | `easeInOut` = `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` |
| A piece's controls arriving (toolbars, captions, hints) | `{...riseIn(delay)}`: up from 12px lower, blur 6px → 0, opacity, 0.55s `easeOut`; stagger parts by ~0.08s. Slow on purpose |
| Icon swaps | `{...iconSwap}`: scale 0.25 → 1, blur 4px → 0, spring 0.3s bounce 0, inside `AnimatePresence mode="popLayout" initial={false}` |
| Text/number swaps | y ±8 to 14px + blur 4px + opacity, ~0.2 to 0.25s `easeOut` |
| Labels changing inside a button | plain opacity crossfade, 150ms linear: no blur, no movement (Anish: "the blur even on text doesn't feel good") |
| Tooltips | 125ms, scale 0.97, delay the first, then instant for neighbours |
| Dropdowns/popovers | ~180ms, scale 0.95 → 1 from the trigger side, exit faster (~100ms) |
| Springs | `{ type: "spring", duration, bounce }`; bounce 0 to 0.2, up to 0.3 only for playful/alive things (dynamic island, swipe cards) |

Rules:
- When swapping content (viewer info, labels), use the direction-aware `swap` (small x drift + blur, exit before enter) or a plain blur crossfade; never slide text vertically with `y`, and keep persistent chrome (install card, pager) out of the swap so it never moves.
- Layouts must not move when content changes height: anchor or center from the viewport (the viewer centers on viewport height minus a constant info allowance), and let trailing controls glide with `layout="position"`.
- UI animations stay under 300ms. Exits are faster than enters.
- Never animate from `scale(0)`; start at 0.5+ with opacity.
- Never animate keyboard-initiated actions (the ⌘K search results open instantly).
- Slow where the user decides, fast where the system responds (hold to confirm: 2s linear press, 200ms release).
- `AnimatePresence initial={false}` for state swaps so nothing animates on page load.
- Drag: pointer capture, velocity counts as much as distance, rubber-band past edges. In the viewer one motion value (`pos`, the fractional piece index) drives every slide's x, scale and opacity, so the next piece grows under the finger; release projects the throw (`pos + velocity * 0.2`), commits past 15% of a piece, and hands the release velocity to the glide spring.
- Reduced motion is handled globally by `<MotionConfig reducedMotion="user">` in `src/app/providers.tsx`.
- Edge cases handled invisibly: toasts pause on hover and when the tab is hidden.
- Optical alignment: nudge play triangles and asymmetric icons.
